## Limitations

- Producer size labels expressed as MB/GB have been preserved without converting to bytes when the publisher did not give exact bytes or clarify the unit convention.
- For ranks 23, 25, 29 and 30, no total is claimed across the multi-layer/family or continuing collection.
- A one-time `curl -I` metadata attempt for the glacier DOI could not resolve `doi.org` from the local shell; source content and size claims were obtained from browser-accessible author/publisher pages instead. No response body was retrieved.
- SoilGrids' per-map public listings are tiled and do not provide an aggregate byte total for the selected six-depth bdod mean bundle within the metadata inspection budget. The one tile inspected was not generalized into a whole-map estimate.
